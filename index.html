About the project

Designed to bridge the gap between career services and real-world preparation

CareerCoach AI was developed as part of the MN5814: Emerging Technologies in Business coursework at Royal Holloway, University of London. The project explores how socially intelligent digital humans can enhance employability training and support student confidence.
